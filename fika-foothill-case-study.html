Fika & Foothill.

Crafted coffee, Toronto roots.

A concept site for a quiet, two-location Toronto coffee roaster — built around warm ivory tones, a deep forest-green anchor, and an editorial serif that lets the coffee (and the people drinking it) stay the focus, not the interface.

Concept & Intent

The thinking
behind the brand.

Fika & Foothill needed to feel like a neighbourhood ritual, not a chain. The palette leans on warm cream and a deep forest green with a small hit of gold — calm enough to read as "quiet corner," confident enough to still feel premium. Editorial serif headlines carry the voice; the rest of the interface stays out of the way.

Structurally, I treated it like a hospitality site rather than a menu app: a founder-story section with layered photography, two location cards with real hours and directions, and a menu that reads like a printed list rather than a database table. The goal was a site that feels hand-built for two specific rooms in Toronto, not templated for anywhere.

How this differs from Altitudes & Golden Eden — both of those lean cool and architectural or dark and gem-lit; Fika & Foothill is the warm, daylight counterpart in the hospitality set — ivory and forest green instead of black and gold, built for a morning ritual instead of an evening one.
