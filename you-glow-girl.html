Chic. Functional. Made for You.

A compact, considered product identity for a premium quilted makeup bag — designed for everyday essentials and finished with gold-tone hardware and quiet, feminine detailing.

Concept & Intent

Designed to glow
from the inside out.

You Glow Girl was designed as a soft, feminine product identity for a premium makeup bag — built around everyday functionality and quiet confidence. The quilted silhouette, gold-tone hardware, and rounded wordmark plate position the brand as considered and elevated without losing its warmth.

This project was an opportunity to practice product-level branding — translating a name and a feeling into material, color, and form, and to demonstrate the ability to carry a brand's tone of voice from packaging language all the way through to the smallest physical details.
