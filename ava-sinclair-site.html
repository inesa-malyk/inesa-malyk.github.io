About

Ten years in front of
the world's best lenses.

Ava began her career at nineteen, walking for three houses during her first Milan season. What followed was a decade split between editorial storytelling and global campaign work — a career built on range rather than repetition.

She's as comfortable under studio strobes as she is on a moving runway, known among photographers for finding stillness inside motion. Off set, she splits her time between Paris and New York.
