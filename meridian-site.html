About Meridian

We started Meridian because most crypto products looked like the chain they were built on — not like the brand they were meant to be.

Meridian is a Web3 product studio founded by engineers and designers who shipped their own protocols before they ever pitched a client. That background shapes how we work: fewer decks, more prototypes; fewer buzzwords, more shipped code.

Full-stack, by design

Brand strategists sit next to smart contract engineers. Every launch is designed and audited by the same team from day one.

Chain-agnostic

Ethereum, Solana, Base, or an app-chain of your own — we choose infrastructure around your product, not the other way around.

Built for scrutiny

We design for the audit, the AMA, and the bear market — not just the launch day chart.
