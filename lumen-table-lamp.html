Lumen
Table Lamp.

A Compact Bedside Lamp Built on Honest Materials.

A minimal product identity for a bedside table lamp — stoneware, brass, and linen brought together for a warm, unhurried light.

Project Type
Personal / Concept
Industry
Home & Lighting
Scope
Product Identity · Material Concept
Concept & Intent

Light, built
from honest material.

Lumen was designed as a compact bedside lamp built on honest materials — glazed stoneware, brushed brass, and unbleached linen — for a warm, unhurried light. The rounded, hand-thrown base and soft drum shade position the brand as considered and quietly confident, at home in a slow, tactile interior.

This project was an opportunity to practice product identity for the home and lighting category — pairing material honesty with restrained typography, and to demonstrate the ability to translate a brand feeling into form, texture, and finish across an entirely different product category.
