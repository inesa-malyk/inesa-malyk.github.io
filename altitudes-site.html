03 · Method

Four movements.

I
Site

A week on the ridge. We measure the wind, the snow line, and where the sun lands at four.

II
Section

We draw in section before plan. Every house begins as a relationship with gravity.

III
Materials

Larch, lime, slate, brass. Sourced within two valleys whenever the geology allows.

IV
Hand-off

Eighteen months from siting to keys. We furnish the first season.
