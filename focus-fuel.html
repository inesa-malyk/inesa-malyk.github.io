Concept & Intent

Fuel the grind.
Focus the mind.

Focus Fuel was designed as a minimal, powerful gym water bottle built for performance, discipline, and everyday motivation. The matte black shell, arrow logomark, and structured typography position the brand as focused and no-nonsense — built for the gym floor, not the shelf.

This project was an opportunity to practice product identity for the sports and fitness industry — applying a single wordmark and logomark consistently across form, color, and material, and to demonstrate range across categories, from apparel and packaging to hard goods.
