Altitudes Atelier.

Architecture for High Places — Geneva & Annecy

A website for a fictional alpine architecture studio designing private residences across Switzerland and France. The brief called for something as restrained and considered as the houses themselves — a site that reads like a section drawing, not a brochure.

Concept & Intent

The thinking
behind the brand.

Altitudes needed to feel like the houses it builds: quiet, exact, unhurried. Every studio doing alpine architecture leans on the same visual shorthand — snow, timber, wide-angle drone shots. I wanted the opposite: a site with the discipline of a section drawing, where structure and coordinates carry as much weight as the photography.

Rather than give Altitudes its own separate visual language, I carried over the same warm, editorial system used across your portfolio — Cormorant Garamond for the display type, Outfit for everything functional, and the same charcoal-and-ivory palette with a soft neutral accent. It keeps every project reading as one continuous body of work rather than a set of disconnected experiments.
